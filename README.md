### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **Information & Communication Technology (ICT)** diploma student passionate about bridging the gap between hardware and software. I love getting hands-on with microcontrollers, developing intelligent desktop & IoT applications, and creating tech content demonstrations.

- 🔭 **Current Focus:** Developing smart automation, robotics, and interactive Python/CustomTkinter desktop software.
- 🏎️ **Robotics Projects:** Built a Wi-Fi-controlled car with obstacle avoidance using **Arduino Uno**, **ESP8266**, and **HC-SR04** ultrasonic sensors with WASD keyboard steering.
- 💼 **Goals:** Actively seeking summer internship opportunities to apply practical engineering skills to real-world products.
- 🎬 **Content Creation:** Scripting and producing short-form video content demonstrating electronic builds, circuit wiring, and 3D printing.
